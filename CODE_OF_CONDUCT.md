# Code of conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: everyone interacting in this repository's issues and discussions is expected to be
respectful, welcoming, and constructive. Harassment, personal attacks, and demeaning conduct
are not acceptable.

## Scope

This applies to issues, discussions, and any other space this project uses, and to anyone
representing the project in public.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer directly. Reports are handled privately and
in confidence. The maintainer is responsible for clarifying standards and may remove
comments, close discussions, or block accounts for behaviour they judge inappropriate.

## A note on disagreement

Disagreeing with a detection is not misconduct, it is the point. These reports publish model
output including its errors, and telling me a card is wrong is the most useful contribution
anyone can make. Argue with the findings freely. Be decent to the people.
