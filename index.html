How to read these

  • Every card is a model detection, not a confirmed sighting, unless it says a person reviewed it. The reports show their false positives rather than hiding them, because a page that only shows successes cannot be judged.
  • Every label is shown only above its own measured error rate, at its own site. Floors are measured per hydrophone, never pooled across them.
  • No detections is not no animals. Each report states its hours of usable signal. A quiet day and an unrecorded day look identical on a count.

Full method, including how false-positive floors are measured and why locations are published only at approximate precision, is in METHODS.md.
