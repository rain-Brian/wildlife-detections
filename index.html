How to read these

  • Every card is a model detection, not a confirmed sighting, unless it says a person reviewed it.
  • Featured hydrophone evidence needs the full 30-second source window. Short review excerpts can remain counted, but they do not lead public evidence.
  • Every label is shown only above its own measured error rate, at its own site. Floors are measured per hydrophone, never pooled across them.
  • No detections is not no animals. Coverage and source-quality gaps are shown because a quiet day and an unrecorded day are not the same thing.

Full method, including false-positive floors, media rights, and approximate locations, is in METHODS.md. Machine-readable site data is in site-data.json.
