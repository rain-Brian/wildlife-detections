Gaps And Limits

  • Coverage is near continuous. Orcasound Lab is the exception, losing about two hours on 2026-08-29 and smaller amounts on 08-30 and 08-31.
  • The three stations disagree enormously. Bush Point returns a positive on 21.8 percent of windows, Orcasound Lab on 1.78 percent, North San Juan Channel on 0.67 percent. The project's own measured false positive floor for this detector is 7.5 percent, with a 95 percent interval from 2.6 to 19.9 percent, and the code that computes it notes it was measured on detector negative windows and so probably understates the true rate. North SJC and Lab therefore sit below that floor and should not be read as presence.
  • Bush Point's detections are close to statistically independent of the other two hydrophones. Only 4.0 percent of its positive minutes coincide with a positive minute elsewhere, against 3.4 percent expected by chance, a ratio of 1.17. Its positives also appear in all 24 hours and 84 percent of consecutive positive windows are adjacent. Six days of continuous presence at one station, largely unheard at the others, is not a conclusion this data supports. These hydrophones are tens of kilometres apart and calls need not carry between them, so this is consistent with a local sound source without proving one.
  • North San Juan Channel may be too quiet rather than genuinely empty. Across a sampled hour its highest window confidence was 0.039. That is worth a separate look at the capture chain, because a hydrophone that cannot hear will never report anything.
  • Nobody has listened to a single clip. The review machinery in reports/verdicts.py exists and has never produced a verdict file. Until it does, there is no precision estimate for this detector on this audio.

These are model outputs for triage and coverage accounting. They are not human-reviewed biological conclusions.
