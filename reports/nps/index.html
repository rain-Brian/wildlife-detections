Where it listens

NPS Sunrise Mountain

46.91°N, 121.64°W

inactive NPS has taken the Sunrise webcams down for the winter: the park webcam page reads 'Down for the winter. Check back summer 2027', and SunriseMtn.jpg has not changed since 2026-09-29 18:04Z (Sunrise and White River roads closed for the season). Lake raw stills end 2026-09-29. Restore by flipping active when NPS resumes the camera.

NPS West

inactive NPS camera feeds deactivated by decision on 2026-10-27, alongside the seasonal Sunrise shutdown; the West camera itself was still updating. Restore by flipping active.

No false-positive floor is recorded in these reports' manifests. A label shown without a measured floor at its own site is a model output, not a rate.

Reports

MegaDetector. Detects that an animal is present, not which species it is.

SpeciesNet. Names the species in a MegaDetector box. At Sunrise its white-tailed deer labels are black-tailed deer, and an unnamed "animal" is usually a person crouching.
